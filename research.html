Research profile

Human-centered autonomy.

My research interest is in designing autonomous and intelligent systems that people can understand, guide, and trust. I am particularly interested in interfaces for communicating human intent, supporting appropriate intervention, and creating effective collaboration between people and robots.

FOCUS 01

Human-guided autonomy

How can people communicate goals, preferences, corrections, and situational knowledge to autonomous systems?

FOCUS 02

Mixed-initiative interaction

How should an intelligent system balance proactive assistance with human agency, oversight, and control?

FOCUS 03

Embodied & immersive systems

How do embodiment, spatial context, and affect shape understanding and trust in interactive systems?

Human-in-the-loop AI products
