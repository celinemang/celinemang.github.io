Research direction

Building agency into intelligent systems.

I am interested in how people communicate intent to autonomous systems, understand their behavior, and retain meaningful control. My background in immersive interaction and human-in-the-loop AI gives me a foundation for studying these questions in HRI.

01

Human-guided autonomy

Interfaces that help people specify goals, preferences, and corrections to autonomous systems.

02

Interaction with AI agents

Mixed-initiative systems that explain recommendations and preserve human approval for consequential actions.

03

Embodied & immersive interaction

Using VR and interactive prototypes to study behavior, affect, spatial understanding, and trust.
